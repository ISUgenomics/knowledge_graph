# Barium isotope dynamics in a ferruginous lake: A window into Precambrian ocean barium cycling

## Abstract

This dataset includes geochemical and Ba isotopic data from the Deming Lake water column across three seasons, along with samples from snow-ice, surrounding bogs, exposed groundwater (spring waters), and a surface sediment depth profile.
