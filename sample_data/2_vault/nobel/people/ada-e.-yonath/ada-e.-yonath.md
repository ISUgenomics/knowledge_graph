# Ada E. Yonath

Ada E. Yonath is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (2009) for Studies of the structure and function of the ribosome. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Weizmann Institute of Science.
