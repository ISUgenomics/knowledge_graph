# Akira Suzuki

Akira Suzuki is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (2010) for Palladium-catalyzed cross couplings in organic synthesis. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Hokkaido University.
