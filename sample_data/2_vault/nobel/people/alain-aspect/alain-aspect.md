# Alain Aspect

Alain Aspect is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (2022) for Experiments with entangled photons, establishing the violation of Bell inequalities and  pioneering quantum information science. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Institut d’Optique Graduate School – Université Paris-Saclay.
