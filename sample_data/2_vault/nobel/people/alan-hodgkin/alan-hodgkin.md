# Alan Hodgkin

Alan Hodgkin is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (1963) for Their discoveries concerning the ionic mechanisms involved in excitation and inhibition in the peripheral and central portions of the nerve cell membrane. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was University of Cambridge.
