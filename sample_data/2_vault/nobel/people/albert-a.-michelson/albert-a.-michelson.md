# Albert A. Michelson

Albert A. Michelson is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (1907) for His optical precision instruments and the spectroscopic and metrological investigations carried out with their aid. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was University of Chicago.
