# Abhijit Banerjee

Abhijit Banerjee is an economist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences (2019) for Their experimental approach to alleviating global poverty. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).
