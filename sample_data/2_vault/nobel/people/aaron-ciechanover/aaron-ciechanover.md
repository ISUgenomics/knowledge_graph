# Aaron Ciechanover

Aaron Ciechanover is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (2004) for The discovery of ubiquitin-mediated protein degradation. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Technion - Israel Institute of Technology.
