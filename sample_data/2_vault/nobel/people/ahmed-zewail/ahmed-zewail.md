# Ahmed Zewail

Ahmed Zewail is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (1999) for His studies of the transition states of chemical reactions using femtosecond spectroscopy. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was California Institute of Technology (Caltech).
