# Alfred Kastler

Alfred Kastler is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (1966) for The discovery and development of optical methods for studying Hertzian resonances in atoms. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was École Normale Supérieure.
