# Alfred Werner

Alfred Werner is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (1913) for In recognition of his work on the linkage of atoms in molecules by which he has thrown new light on earlier investigations and opened up new fields of research especially in inorganic chemistry. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was University of Zurich.
