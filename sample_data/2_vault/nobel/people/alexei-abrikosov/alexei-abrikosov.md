# Alexei Abrikosov

Alexei Abrikosov is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (2003) for Pioneering contributions to the theory of superconductors and superfluids. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Argonne National Laboratory.
