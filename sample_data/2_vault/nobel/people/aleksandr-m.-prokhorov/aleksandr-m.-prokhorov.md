# Aleksandr M. Prokhorov

Aleksandr M. Prokhorov is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (1964) for Fundamental work in the field of quantum electronics, which has led to the construction of oscillators and amplifiers based on the maser-laser principle. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was P.N. Lebedev Physical Institute.
