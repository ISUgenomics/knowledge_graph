# Adolf Windaus

Adolf Windaus is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (1928) for The services rendered through his research into the constitution of the sterols and their connection with the vitamins. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Goettingen University.
