# Alfred D. Hershey

Alfred D. Hershey is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (1969) for Their discoveries concerning the replication mechanism and the genetic structure of viruses. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Carnegie Institution of Washington.
