# Alfred G. Gilman

Alfred G. Gilman is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (1994) for Their discovery of G-proteins and the role of these proteins in signal transduction in cells. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center at Dallas.
