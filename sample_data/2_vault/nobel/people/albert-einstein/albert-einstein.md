# Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (1921) for His services to Theoretical Physics, and especially for his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Kaiser-Wilhelm-Institut (now Max-Planck-Institut) für Physik.
