# Aaron Klug

Aaron Klug is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (1982) for His development of crystallographic electron microscopy and his structural elucidation of biologically important nucleic acid-protein complexes. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology.
