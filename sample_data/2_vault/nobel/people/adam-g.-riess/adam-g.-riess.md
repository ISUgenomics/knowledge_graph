# Adam G. Riess

Adam G. Riess is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (2011) for The discovery of the accelerating expansion of the Universe through observations of distant supernovae. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Johns Hopkins University.
