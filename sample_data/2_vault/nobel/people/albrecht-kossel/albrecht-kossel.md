# Albrecht Kossel

Albrecht Kossel is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (1910) for In recognition of the contributions to our knowledge of cell chemistry made through his work on proteins, including the nucleic substances.
