# Adolf Butenandt

Adolf Butenandt is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (1939) for His work on sex hormones. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was Kaiser-Wilhelm-Institut (now Max-Planck-Institut) für Biochemie.
