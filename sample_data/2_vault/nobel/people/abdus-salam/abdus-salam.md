# Abdus Salam

Abdus Salam is a scientist and Nobel laureate associated with the Nobel Prize in Physics (1979) for Their contributions to the theory of the unified weak and electromagnetic interaction between elementary particles, including, inter alia, the prediction of the weak neutral current. At the time of the award, the recorded affiliation was International Centre for Theoretical Physics.
