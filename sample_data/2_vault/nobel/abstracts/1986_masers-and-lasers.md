# Masers and lasers

## Abstract

Shortly before his death in January 1984 Professor Alfred Kastler, who won the 1966 Nobel physics prize for his work on optical pumping, wrote an extensive book review of Masers and Lasers by Mario Bertolotti (1983 Bristo1:Adam Hilger). A shortened version appeared in Nature (1985 316 307-9). This is the full version.
