# Lucas Showman

Lucas Showman is a manager in Iowa State University's Office of Biotechnology, leading the W.M. Keck Metabolomics Research Laboratory. Public scholarly profiles connect this work to Gut microbiota and health, Neuroscience and Neuropharmacology Research, and Ruminant Nutrition and Digestive Physiology.
