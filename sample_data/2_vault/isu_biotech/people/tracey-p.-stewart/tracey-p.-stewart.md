# Tracey P. Stewart

Tracey P. Stewart is a manager in Iowa State University's Office of Biotechnology, leading the High Resolution Microscopy Facility. Public scholarly profiles connect this work to Plant Virus Research Studies, Plant Pathogens and Resistance, and Plant Disease Resistance and Genetics.
