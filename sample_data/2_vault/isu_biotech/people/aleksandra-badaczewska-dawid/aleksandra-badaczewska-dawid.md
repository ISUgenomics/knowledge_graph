# Aleksandra Badaczewska-Dawid

Aleksandra Badaczewska-Dawid is a staff member in Iowa State University's Office of Biotechnology, supporting the Genome Informatics Facility. Public scholarly profiles connect this work to Protein Structure and Dynamics, Enzyme Structure and Function, and Chemical Synthesis and Analysis.
