# Andrew Severin

Andrew Severin is a manager in Iowa State University's Office of Biotechnology, leading the Genome Informatics Facility. Public scholarly profiles connect this work to Legume Nitrogen Fixing Symbiosis, Soybean genetics and cultivation, and Plant Pathogens and Fungal Diseases.
