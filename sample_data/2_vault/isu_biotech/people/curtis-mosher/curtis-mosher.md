# Curtis Mosher

Curtis Mosher is a manager in Iowa State University's Office of Biotechnology, leading the High Resolution Microscopy Facility. Public scholarly profiles connect this work to Advanced Biosensing Techniques and Applications, Force Microscopy Techniques and Applications, and Nanofabrication and Lithography Techniques.
