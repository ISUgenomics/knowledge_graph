# Rick Masonbrink

Rick Masonbrink is a staff member in Iowa State University's Office of Biotechnology, supporting the Genome Informatics Facility. Public scholarly profiles connect this work to Plant-Microbe Interactions and Immunity, Nematode management and characterization studies, and Plant nutrient uptake and metabolism.
