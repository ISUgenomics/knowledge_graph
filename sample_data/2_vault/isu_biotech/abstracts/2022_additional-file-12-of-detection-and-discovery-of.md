# Additional file 12 of Detection and discovery of plant viruses in soybean by metagenomic sequencing

## Abstract

Additional file 12 Table S2. Sample location and virus sequence identified from field-grown soybean samples collected across the United States of America. The viruses were identified from the all-organism NCBI nucleotide and protein databases through BLASTN and BLASTX searches using Blastplus v2.7.1. The virus identified for each sample had high percent nucleotide identity (&gt; 99%) to the corresponding virus isolates identified in this study
