# Metabolite Profiling of Methanosarcina Acetivorans (MA) with Direct Infusion to FT-ICR

## Abstract

Methane producing organisms are of interest because they provide an efficient and cost-effective biofuel that can be distributed readily using existing infrastructure. Methanosarcina acetivorans (MA) is a methanogenic archaeon found in diverse anaerobic habitats including oil wells, trash dumps, deep sea hydrothermal vents, and oxygen depleted sediments beneath kelp beds. With the largest genome of all known methanogenic archaea containing 4721 predicted genes; MA is considered to be the most metabolically diverse methanogenic archaea.
