# Notes Journal

Aug 5, 2026

This is definitely a work in progress. I originally conceived of this idea after exploring Andrej Karpathy's tweet about LLM wikis and everyone jumping on the bandwagon using obsidian.  I found the obsidian interface to be inadequate for data exploration and wanted to be able to do more than just pull on force graphed nodes. I also preferred 3D visualization.  As I got into making my own version, I decided the power of being able to compute over the data was immensely powerful and converted the wiki part into a database with auto tag generation and tag resolution and tag ontology. The original use case was for a meeting I was presenting at. I inputed all the information the organizers provided about the conference and then generated tags and was able to ask questions about who I should prioritize meeting at the event.  Then explored creating a database of AI researchers at Iowa State to get a feel of what areas are currently actively being researched.  Turns out its biomedical, plant sciences, genomics, agriculture, molecular biology, materials science, education, renewable energy, cybersecurity, bioinformatics, computer vision, nanotechnology and more.  The 3D graph showed who collaborated with whom and distinct clusters of researchers. Since, I have found it useful, I am working to make it available to others and trying to apply it to biological datasets.  

This is still very much a work in progress.
